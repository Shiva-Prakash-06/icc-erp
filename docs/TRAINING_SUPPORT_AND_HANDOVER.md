# Training, Support, and Leadership Handover

## Role-based training

- **OIA/System administrators:** accounts, scoped assignments, controlled vocabularies, imports, audits, retention, incidents, deployment and recovery.
- **Faculty coordinators:** approvals, waivers, sensitive references, closure, reports, and escalation.
- **ICC Secretary/heads:** annual rosters, projects, schedules, tasks, attendance, contributions, budgets, feedback, reports, and year-end handover.
- **IGP heads/leads:** partners, cohorts, recruitment, logistics, checklists, buddies, restricted-reference policy, and program closure.
- **Associates/volunteers/buddies:** assigned work, attendance, contributions, interactions, concerns, and personal notifications.
- **Participants:** permitted schedule/resources and feedback.
- **Auditors:** read-only filters, snapshot provenance, and audit interpretation.

Each session must use staging, complete the role's critical journey, and record attendee, trainer, date, result, and follow-up need.

## Support model

- Severity 1: active security/privacy incident, total outage, or data corruption. Immediate technical and OIA escalation.
- Severity 2: critical workflow unavailable with no workaround. Response within four working hours.
- Severity 3: degraded workflow with workaround. Triage within one working day.
- Severity 4: question or enhancement. Backlog review.

Support records include environment, request ID, affected role/scope/project, reproduction steps, business impact, classification, owner, resolution, and closure approval. Sensitive file content must never be attached.

## Annual leadership handover

1. Confirm the closing roster and expire outgoing assignments on the approved term date.
2. Resolve active projects, tasks, approvals, risks, checklists, documents, budgets, and escalations.
3. Generate the academic-year and handover reports.
4. Create the new governance term and position structure without copying people automatically.
5. Assign and approve incoming leaders with effective dates.
6. Verify outgoing access is revoked and offline snapshots are invalidated.
7. Preserve historical rosters, reports, audits, and contribution history.

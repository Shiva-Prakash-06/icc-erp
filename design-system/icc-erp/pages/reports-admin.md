# Reports, People, Upload a sheet, History and Alerts

Inherits `MASTER.md`.

- **Reports** (Faculty, USC): five numbers for a year — events, people,
  volunteer hours, money paid, feedback replies — then the finished events
  with their PDFs.
- **People** (Faculty): find someone, press **Change**, pick what they are,
  save. The year follows from the role; the campus is asked only when there
  is more than one.
- **Upload a sheet**: get the empty sheet, fill it in, upload it. The rows
  are checked first and nothing is added until someone presses **Save** —
  and only Faculty can. The USC sees "Waiting for Faculty to save it".
- **Who changed what**: one sentence per change — who, did what, when. The
  raw identifiers sit behind each row's "⋯".
- **Alerts**: newest first, five at a time, each opening what it is about.
  One switch: "Email me when something needs me".
- Printed pages are white, without the rail.
